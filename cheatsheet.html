Chapter 6 — Front Joint · Independence · Sums · Conditionals

Joint (§6.1)

Joint CDF
$$F(x,y)=P(X\le x,\,Y\le y)$$
  • $F(-\infty,y)=F(x,-\infty)=0$
  • $F(\infty,\infty)=1$
  • $P(a_1{<}X{\le}a_2,\,b_1{<}Y{\le}b_2)=F(a_2,b_2){-}F(a_1,b_2){-}F(a_2,b_1){+}F(a_1,b_1)$
Joint PMF (discrete)
$$p(x,y)=P(X{=}x,Y{=}y)$$ Marginals: $p_X(x)=\sum_y p(x,y)$
Joint PDF (continuous)
$$P((X,Y)\in A)=\iint_A f(x,y)\,dx\,dy$$ Marginals: $f_X(x)=\int f(x,y)\,dy$
$f(x,y)=\dfrac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x\,\partial y}$

Independence (§6.2)

Factorization
$X\perp Y \iff F(x,y)=F_X(x)F_Y(y)$
Equivalently: $f(x,y)=f_X(x)f_Y(y)$
Lemma: If $f(x,y)=g(x)h(y)$ on a rectangular support, then indep.
Trap
Support NOT rectangular ⇒ NOT independent, even if $f$ factors.
Example: $f(x,y)=2$ on $0<x<y<1$ — looks like $2\cdot 1$ but $x<y$ couples them.
Consequences
  • $E[XY]=E[X]E[Y]$
  • $\mathrm{Var}(X{+}Y)=\mathrm{Var}(X){+}\mathrm{Var}(Y)$
  • $M_{X+Y}(t)=M_X(t)M_Y(t)$

Sums & Convolution (§6.3)

Convolution
Discrete: $p_{X+Y}(a)=\sum_k p_X(k)\,p_Y(a{-}k)$
Continuous: $f_{X+Y}(a)=\int f_X(x)\,f_Y(a{-}x)\,dx$
Integrate only where both densities are nonzero.

MGF Trick
If $X\perp Y$: $M_{X+Y}(t)=M_X(t)M_Y(t)$.
Recognize the product → identify the sum distribution.
Expectation/Variance
$E[aX+bY]=aE[X]+bE[Y]$ (always)
$\mathrm{Var}(aX{+}bY)=a^2\mathrm{Var}(X){+}b^2\mathrm{Var}(Y){+}2ab\,\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$
If indep: Cov = 0.

Conditionals (§6.4–6.5)

Conditional PMF / PDF
$$p_{X|Y}(x|y)=\dfrac{p(x,y)}{p_Y(y)} \quad \text{(discrete)}$$ $$f_{X|Y}(x|y)=\dfrac{f(x,y)}{f_Y(y)} \quad \text{(continuous)}$$ For fixed $y$: $\sum_x p_{X|Y}=1$ or $\int f_{X|Y}dx=1$.
Conditional Expectation
$$E[X|Y{=}y]=\sum_x x\,p_{X|Y}(x|y)$$ $$E[X|Y{=}y]=\int x\,f_{X|Y}(x|y)\,dx$$ $E[X|Y]$ is a random variable (function of $Y$).
Law of Total Expectation
$$E[X]=E\big[E[X|Y]\big]$$ Discrete: $E[X]=\sum_y E[X|Y{=}y]\,p_Y(y)$
Cts: $E[X]=\int E[X|Y{=}y]\,f_Y(y)\,dy$
Law of Total Variance
$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E[\mathrm{Var}(X|Y)]+\mathrm{Var}(E[X|Y])$$ "Average of the variances plus variance of the averages."
Independence recap
If $X\perp Y$: $p_{X|Y}(x|y)=p_X(x)$, $f_{X|Y}(x|y)=f_X(x)$, $E[X|Y]=E[X]$.

Change of Variables (§6.6)

Univariate (monotone $g$)
$$f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))\left|\dfrac{d}{dy}g^{-1}(y)\right|$$
Bivariate Jacobian
Given $U=g_1(X,Y)$, $V=g_2(X,Y)$, solve for $X=h_1(U,V)$, $Y=h_2(U,V)$. $$J=\det\!\begin{pmatrix} \partial x/\partial u & \partial x/\partial v \\ \partial y/\partial u & \partial y/\partial v \end{pmatrix}$$ $$f_{U,V}(u,v)=f_{X,Y}(h_1,h_2)\,|J|$$ (Or equivalently divide by $|J_{X\to UV}|$ — same number, whichever you compute.)
4-Step Recipe
  1. Write $U=g_1, V=g_2$.
  2. Solve for $X,Y$ in terms of $U,V$.
  3. Compute $|J|$.
  4. Find new support (image of original region).

Order Statistics

Joint density of all order stats
$$f_{X_{(1)},\dots,X_{(n)}}(x_1,\dots,x_n)=n!\prod_i f(x_i)$$ on $x_1<x_2<\dots<x_n$.
$k$-th order stat
$$f_{X_{(k)}}(x)=\dfrac{n!}{(k{-}1)!(n{-}k)!}F(x)^{k-1}(1{-}F(x))^{n-k}f(x)$$ $X_{(1)}=\min$, $X_{(n)}=\max$.

Exam Playbook

Joint density on region (Q1-style)
  1. Sketch support → set integration bounds.
  2. Normalize: $\iint f = 1$ → solve for constant.
  3. Marginal: integrate out the other variable.
  4. Probability: integrate $f$ over the event region.
  5. Independence: check if support is a rectangle AND $f$ factors.
Jacobian (Q2-style)
  1. Pick $U,V$ that simplify the geometry.
  2. Invert to $X(U,V), Y(U,V)$.
  3. Compute $|J|$.
  4. Map support.
  5. Write $f_{U,V}$ and integrate.
Discrete triangular (Q3-style)
  1. Find $C$ from $\sum\sum p(n,m)=1$.
  2. Use $k=n{-}m$ or similar substitution to simplify sums.
  3. Recall $\sum_{n=1}^N n=\tfrac{N(N+1)}{2}$, $\sum n^2=\tfrac{N(N+1)(2N+1)}{6}$.
  4. Conditional: $P(A|B)=P(A{\cap}B)/P(B)$.
CDF→E[X] (Q4-style)
  1. Differentiate $F$ to get $f$.
  2. Recognize $f$ as known (Exp, Γ, Rayleigh, …).
  3. $E[X]=\int x f(x)\,dx$. Substitution $u=x^2$ for Rayleigh-ish.
  4. Reduce to Gamma: $\int_0^\infty u^{s-1}e^{-u}du=\Gamma(s)$.
Traps checklist
  • ☐ Is support rectangular?
  • ☐ Did I take $|J|$ not $J$?
  • ☐ Did I update the support after transformation?
  • ☐ Did I square the density-variable change for CDF/PDF conversion?
  • ☐ Is the denominator in conditional $> 0$?
